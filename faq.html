CloFix WAF is an AI-powered Web Application Firewall that protects web applications, APIs, and digital services from OWASP Top 10 threats, bots, and zero-day attacks using multi-engine inspection (Lua, JS, WASM) and real-time anomaly detection.

CloFix sits between your users and servers, analyzing HTTP/HTTPS traffic in real-time. It uses signature matching, behavioral AI, TLS fingerprinting, and custom rules to block malicious requests before they reach your origin.
