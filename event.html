About The Webinar

CloFix 5-Day Webinar: DevOps & Cybersecurity Knowledge Sharing

Technology is not only about writing code, configuring servers, or learning tools. It is also about understanding how real teams work, how decisions are made, how systems are built, how problems are solved, and how security becomes part of everyday engineering.

At CloFix Infotech, we believe that students and early-career engineers deserve an opportunity to see that world before they step into it.

That is why we are organizing this 5-day knowledge-sharing webinar completely free of cost as part of our social responsibility initiative.

This webinar is designed especially for students, fresh graduates, interns, and junior engineers who want to understand the practical side of DevOps, Cybersecurity, Cloud, Automation, and modern software engineering practices.

Over these five days, we go beyond theory and share insights from real-world engineering environments - how teams collaborate, how development and operations work together, how security is considered, how incidents are handled, and what professional life in technology actually looks like.

You may not leave these five days knowing everything. But our hope is that you leave with something more valuable:

A clearer picture of where you are, where you can go, and what it takes to get there.

No registration fee No hidden cost 5 days of learning

Just five days of learning, sharing, questions, experiences, and conversations.

Because sometimes, all a young engineer needs is someone to show them what lies beyond the classroom.
